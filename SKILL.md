## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a saved cultural souvenir, not an ordinary illustration. Compress the subject into a small commemorative stamp, a cultural seal or a miniature narrative window. Use simple geometry, flat color, silhouette, a few engraved lines and, when useful, a perforation, a frame or a badge contour. Keep engraved grain, screen-print texture, aged paper and ink unevenness. The subject stays restrained inside a large field. Reject 3D, gradients, realistic light and complex detail.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.
